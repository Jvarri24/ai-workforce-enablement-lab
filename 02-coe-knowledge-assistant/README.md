# COE Knowledge Assistant

AI-assisted knowledge retrieval prototype designed to help employees quickly access guidance, procedures, job aids, and operational resources through a centralized conversational experience.

## Overview

Organizations often maintain large collections of documentation, standard operating procedures, job aids, and support resources across multiple systems. As information grows, employees may struggle to locate accurate guidance quickly, resulting in reduced efficiency, inconsistent processes, and increased support requests.

The COE Knowledge Assistant demonstrates how AI-enabled knowledge retrieval can improve information access and support workforce enablement by providing a centralized conversational interface for organizational resources.

Built using synthetic content and fictional business scenarios, this prototype simulates an enterprise knowledge support experience.

## Key Capabilities

### Knowledge Retrieval

Provides access to organizational guidance including:

- Standard operating procedures
- Job aids and reference materials
- Process documentation
- Governance resources
- Frequently asked questions

### Conversational Assistance

Supports natural language interactions that help users:

- Locate relevant information
- Understand organizational processes
- Navigate support resources
- Access operational guidance

### Workforce Enablement

Demonstrates how AI-assisted support can:

- Reduce time spent searching for information
- Improve consistency of guidance
- Support employee self-service
- Increase operational efficiency
